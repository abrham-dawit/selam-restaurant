Menu

Most dishes are served family-style on a shared platter of injera — that's the way it's meant to be eaten. Ask us about vegetarian and fasting (tsom) options; half the menu is naturally plant-based.

Our Story

“Selam” means peace — and that's the feeling we cook for. Our kitchen follows the recipes Eritrean and Ethiopian families have passed down for generations: berbere ground with real heat, niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter) made in-house, and injera fermented the slow way so it comes out soft and properly sour.

Come hungry, eat with your hands, and stay for the bun — the traditional coffee ceremony, roasted and brewed at your table.
